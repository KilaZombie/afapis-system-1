# AFAPIS Portfolio Opportunity Evaluation
Date: 2026-11-08
Scope: four candidate systems; zero personal out-of-pocket cost is mandatory.
Status: evidence-based first-pass evaluation. No income is established and no platform account has been opened or published to.

## Decision summary
Retain System 1 (Phoenix local-service lead generation) as the lead candidate. Advance digital products as the next test candidate. Keep KDP as a low-cost distribution channel for original useful content. Treat affiliate marketing as a later monetization layer rather than the primary income engine because typical commissions are low and traffic is required.

## Verified platform facts
- Amazon KDP says it is free to use; e-books have 35% and eligible 70% royalty options. The 70% option has territory, price, and delivery-cost conditions. Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/earn and https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500
- Amazon.com e-book 70% list-price range was expanded to $2.99–$12.99 effective July 7, 2026. Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560
- Payhip offers a $0/month Free Forever plan with a 5% transaction fee. Stripe/PayPal processing fees also apply. Source: https://payhip.com/pricing
- Gumroad charges 10% + $0.50 for direct/profile sales and 30% for marketplace-discovery sales, so it is less attractive for low-priced products. Source: https://gumroad.com/pricing
- Amazon Associates standard commission rates vary by category; the current table lists 3% for Home Improvement, Home, Lawn & Garden, and several adjacent categories. Source: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ
- Google AdSense requires original, high-quality content that attracts an audience; it is not an immediate monetization shortcut. Source: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724

## Recommended portfolio sequence
1. System 1: continue contractor-buyer validation and free lead-routing build.
2. System 2 candidate: digital products. Build one genuinely useful product and validate its demand before making a catalog.
3. System 3 candidate: KDP publishing. Repackage only original, high-value material into a distinct book; do not publish thin or duplicate content.
4. System 4 candidate: affiliate content. Build only after useful content and audience are established; use clear affiliate disclosures and track actual conversions.

These are recommendations, not confirmed workspace assignments. System 2–4 remain unassigned until the user confirms or the Master Project Record contains a later decision.

## Income expectations
No defensible dollar forecast can be made before actual traffic, conversion, buyer acceptance, and sales data exist. Do not multiply arbitrary views by assumed conversion rates and call the result expected income. Report actual cleared revenue, platform fees, refunds, and net margin.

## Zero-cost execution boundary
No paid domains, ads, subscriptions, trials, software, or other personal expenditures. Use only services with a verified $0 entry tier, and account for transaction fees only after a sale. Do not create accounts, accept legal terms, submit tax/payment details, publish publicly, or make binding business commitments on the user's behalf without the required user action/approval.
