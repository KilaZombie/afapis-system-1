# System 1 contractor pilot
Prepared: 2026-11-08
Status: internal draft only. No contractor has been contacted and no agreement exists.

## Goal
Secure one qualified Phoenix-metro contractor willing to test a small batch of exclusive, consented dryer-vent and/or air-duct service requests. Prove the full path from visitor request to contractor contact, booked job, accepted lead, and collected revenue before scaling.

## Why start with dryer-vent requests
Phoenix consumer cost data from Angi (updated July 8, 2026) places typical dryer-vent cleaning at $107–$156, average $131. The service is narrower and easier to qualify than whole-home duct cleaning. Air-duct requests can be a higher-ticket second category, but need more careful qualification and accurate claims.
Source: https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-dryer-vent-cleaning-cost/az/phoenix

## Pilot structure to propose
- One contractor buyer at a time; no lead resale or multi-buyer distribution.
- Contractor defines covered ZIP codes, accepted job types, operating hours, and capacity.
- Lead includes only customer-submitted details needed to respond: name, phone, ZIP, requested service, timing, and consent timestamp/source.
- Customer is clearly told the request will be shared with the selected provider before submitting.
- Contractor agrees to contact the customer promptly and report status: accepted, contacted, booked, completed, invalid, or disputed.
- Start with a small test batch and no upfront payment.
- Trial pricing hypothesis: test $20–$35 per accepted exclusive dryer-vent lead, or a fixed referral fee per completed job if the contractor prefers. These are negotiation hypotheses, not verified market rates.
- Define invalid lead rules before sending any real lead. Examples: wrong service area, disconnected number, duplicate within agreed window, spam, or no service requested.
- Refund/credit only according to written agreed criteria.
- No payment is collected from the consumer by the lead-gen site. The contractor quotes, contracts with, and bills the customer directly.

## Qualification questions for a contractor
1. Do you currently perform dryer-vent cleaning, air-duct cleaning, or both?
2. Which ZIP codes do you cover?
3. How many additional jobs can you accept weekly?
4. What is your normal response time for new inquiries?
5. Are you properly licensed for the work you perform and able to provide current insurance evidence?
6. Do you accept exclusive leads from a third-party referral source?
7. Would you test a small batch with no setup fee and pay only for leads meeting a written definition?
8. Which model is preferred: per accepted lead or per completed job?
9. What information do you need to quote and schedule the customer?
10. Who handles complaints, refunds, cancellations, and customer data requests?
