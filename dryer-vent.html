DRYER VENTS

Dryer vent cleaning in Phoenix

A blocked or restricted vent can reduce dryer performance and increase heat buildup. Local service pricing varies by vent length, access, roof termination, and condition.

Common reasons homeowners request service

  • Clothes take longer than normal to dry.
  • The dryer or laundry area becomes unusually hot.
  • Airflow at the exterior termination is weak.
  • The vent has not been cleaned for a long time.
  • A dryer installer or technician recommends inspection or cleaning.

What affects the price?

Phoenix consumer pricing data published by Angi in July 2026 puts typical dryer-vent cleaning at about $107–$156, with an average around $131, according to Angi's July 2026 Phoenix data. Difficult roof-access jobs can cost substantially more.

Questions to ask a provider

  • Will you clean the entire vent path, including the exterior termination?
  • Is roof access required?
  • Is the quoted price for the complete job?
  • Will you show what was removed or found?
  • Are you insured and properly licensed for the work you perform?
