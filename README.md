# AFAPIS System 1 — Phoenix local-service lead generation

System 1 is a Phoenix-metro lead-generation prototype focused on dryer-vent cleaning first, with air-duct cleaning as a second category.

## Current verified state (2026-11-08)
- Static website prototype exists.
- Educational pages cover dryer vents and air ducts.
- The current request form is a prototype and stores test data in the visitor's browser only.
- No contractor receives submissions.
- No contractor buyer or written lead agreement exists.
- No payment flow, live lead routing, analytics, or verified revenue exists.
- Netlify project currently requires team SSO; public launch has not been authorized.
- No confirmed published production deploy was present at the last project check.

## Non-negotiable cost rule
No personal out-of-pocket spending. Do not buy domains, subscriptions, paid trials, advertising, software, services, or incur any financial obligation. Verify free-tier limits and billing settings before use. Stop if a capability cannot be delivered without personal spending.

## Before public launch
1. Confirm the legal requirements for the exact services and vet each provider.
2. Replace placeholder privacy/terms with reviewed production copy.
3. Connect the form to a verified $0-personal-cost backend and lead-delivery method.
4. Implement clear recipient disclosure and auditable consent.
5. Recruit and vet at least one contractor buyer.
6. Agree in writing on lead definition, pricing, service area, capacity, response time, refunds, disputes, and data handling.
7. Add analytics and lead outcome tracking.
8. Run end-to-end tests with synthetic data.
9. Obtain explicit user approval before removing Netlify SSO or publishing publicly.

## Acquisition and content rules
- Do not create a Google Business Profile for this lead-generation entity. Google explicitly lists lead-generation agents or companies as ineligible.
- Do not use unsolicited automated calls or texts.
- Do not mass-publish thin AI pages.
- Do not make unsupported claims that duct cleaning prevents illness or should be done routinely.
- Make clear that this site is an independent referral/lead service, not the contractor.

## Revenue validation
Illustrative arithmetic is not a forecast. No lead price, buyer acceptance rate, conversion rate, or monthly revenue has been validated. Count revenue only after a contractor pays and the funds clear. Report gross revenue, platform/payment fees, refunds, and net margin separately.
