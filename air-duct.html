AIR DUCTS

Air duct cleaning in Phoenix

Homeowners may seek duct cleaning after remodeling, visible contamination, persistent dust concerns, pest activity, or when a qualified HVAC professional recommends inspection.

Before paying for cleaning

  • Ask exactly which components are included.
  • Ask whether the company will inspect the system before recommending work.
  • Get the total price and any extra-access charges in writing.
  • Ask about licensing, insurance, and the company's process.
  • Avoid claims that every home automatically needs frequent duct cleaning.
